What is Vocably?

Vocably is created to help you use a new language in everyday life, because everyday use is the best and most natural form of learning.

Vocably is a browser extension and a mobile app. It works like a dictionary. You can use it to translate and learn every new word you see or hear in your life.

Every time you meet a new English word, try translating it with Vocably and adding it to your collection. Review your words at least once a day, and within two or three months you'll see a real improvement in your English.

Not sure where to start with everyday language use? Here are some ideas:

  • Read the news in English. BBC, Reuters, and The Guardian are all good places to start.
  • Make a habit of visiting English-language websites. Sites like Lifehacker and Reddit offer plenty of interesting content on all kinds of topics.
  • Watch English YouTube channels. I personally like @kurzgesagt — they make high-quality videos with clear, easy-to-follow English.

Do you want to improve faster? Change the language of your AI assistant to English. I wrote a guide about this. You can read it here: https://vocably.pro/ai.html. Give it two or three months, and you'll be surprised by the results.
